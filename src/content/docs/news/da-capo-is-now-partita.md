The notation editor you knew as Da Capo is now called **Partita**.

## Why

Another free music notation editor, [DaCapo Editor](https://github.com/man-schu/dacapoeditor), was already using the name. Two free score editors with the same name would confuse everyone, and they were there first. So we chose a name of our own while the project is young.

A *partita* is a Baroque suite of pieces, like Bach's partitas for solo violin. It is also a close cousin of *partitura*, the Spanish and Italian word for a score.

## What changes for you

- **Nothing is lost.** The next update installs Partita and brings over your downloaded sounds and any unsaved work from Da Capo.
- **Your files still open.** New scores are saved as `.partita`, and `.dacapo` files open as before.
- **New addresses.** The code lives at [github.com/partita-notation/partita](https://github.com/partita-notation/partita) and this website at partita-notation.github.io.
- **On Windows**, Partita installs next to Da Capo. Once Partita runs, you can uninstall Da Capo from Settings → Apps.

What does not change: Partita is free, open source and complete, with no trial, no paid edition and no subscription. Free, forever.
