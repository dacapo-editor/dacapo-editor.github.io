Partita 0.3.14 is a big step between 0.3 and 0.4: a score can now carry every word it needs, its repeats and jumps play the way they are written, and the tools have moved out of the way of the music. Get it from the [download page](/download/), or let your installed copy update itself.

## Every word in its place

- **Titles, header and footer**: subtitle, lyricist or arranger, dedication, part and copyright on page 1, a running header, a footer and page numbers, with `{page}`, `{pages}`, `{title}` and `{composer}` filled in for each page. Click any of them on the page to change it.
- **Tempo markings** such as *Andante con moto ♩ = 76*, printed or hidden, and tempo changes later in the piece that playback follows.
- **Words in the music**: expressions (*dolce*), techniques (*pizz.*) and rehearsal marks.
- **Text boxes** anywhere on a page, with the **T** tool.
- **Text styles**: the font, size, bold and italic of every kind of text.

## Repeats and jumps that play

- **Barlines**: double, final and dashed.
- **Repeats**, played as many times as they say, and **first and second endings**, each played on its own time through.
- **D.C., D.S., segno, coda and Fine**, with *al Fine* and *al Coda*. The playhead goes back with the music.
- **Pickup measures**: a short first measure is not numbered, and the next one is measure 1.
- **System and page breaks** where you want the line or the page to end.

## Room for the music

The palette is gone. The tools sit in **thin, named bars** around the score: what you write with on the left, what goes on the notes on the right, the rest in two rows on top. Every bar can be moved: drag it anywhere around the score, or start a new row or column for it. **View → Toolbars** hides the ones you do not need and saves your arrangements as layouts; **Reset Toolbars** brings back Partita's.

## Also new

- **Spanish**: the whole interface is translated. **View → Language** chooses English, Español or your computer's language.
- **The menus** are organized in categories with submenus, every item on one line, and a tooltip on each.
- **A staff can become a grand staff**: choose a piano for it, and the bass staff is added below.

Everything is described in the [user guide](/guide/structure/), and the full list of changes is in the [changelog](https://github.com/partita-notation/partita/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

**A note on compatibility:** scores that use anything new in this version are saved as file format 7, which Partita 0.3.0 can't open; other scores are saved as before. Unsaved work from 0.3.0 is brought back after the update, as always.
