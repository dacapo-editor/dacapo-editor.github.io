Da Capo 0.2 is the first version you can simply download and install, on Windows, macOS and Linux. Get it from the [download page](/download/).

## What you can do

- **Write for several staves and voices**, with chords, rests, ties, slurs, articulations and dynamics, in any key and time signature.
- **Beams** that follow the time signature: eighths in 4/4 grouped by half bar, dotted-quarter groups in 6/8, and proper secondary beams down to 128th notes.
- **Grab a note and drag it** up or down to change its pitch. In a chord, only the note you grab moves.
- **The eraser**: aim the crosshair at one note — or one tone of a chord — and click. Drag a box to erase a whole passage. One Ctrl+Z brings it all back.
- **Breves and 128th notes**, for early music and fast passages, from View → Breve and 128th Notes.
- **Playback** with the FluidR3 GM instruments: press Space, and hear your notes as you write them.
- **Page view**, wrap view and one-line view, zoom and printing.

## Never lose your work

Every change is written to disk within half a second. After a crash or a power cut, Da Capo offers your music back the next time it starts.

## Updates without hunting for them

From now on, Da Capo tells you when a new version is out, downloads it in the background and installs it when you restart. You can turn this off in the Help menu.

## A note on security warnings

Our Windows installers are not signed yet, so Windows may show a SmartScreen warning: click **More info → Run anyway**. On macOS, the first time, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. We are working on signing the installers.

## What's next
