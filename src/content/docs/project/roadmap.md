Da Capo is in early development. This page summarizes the plan; the detailed, always up-to-date list lives in [ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/dacapo-editor/dacapo/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md) on GitHub.

## Works today

- Multi-staff scores (treble, bass, alto and tenor clefs) with four voices per staff.
- Time and key signatures that can change mid-score.
- Note entry with the keyboard or the mouse, chords, rests, accidentals and transposition.
- Word-processor editing: caret, selection across staves, cut/copy/paste, grouped undo and redo.
- Ties, slurs, articulations and dynamics.
- Page, wrap and continuous views, zoom, printing, light and dark themes.
- Measure rests, multi-measure rests, and measures with too many beats flagged in red.
- Playback with recorded instruments, following ties, articulations and dynamics.
- Several documents open at once, in tabs.
- Crash recovery that brings back unsaved work and its undo history.

## Coming next

- **Notation**: beaming, tuplets, hairpins, tempo marks and text.
- **Workflow**: a new score wizard with instrument templates, insert and overwrite modes, and a note property panel.
- **Formats**: MusicXML import and export, PDF export, recent files.
- **Sound**: an instrument per staff, metronome and count-in, MIDI keyboard input.
- **Interface**: customizable shortcuts and a Spanish translation.

## Further ahead

- **Version history**: go back to earlier states of a score, for example how it looked yesterday.
- Orchestral scores and part extraction, lyrics and chord symbols, guitar tablature.
