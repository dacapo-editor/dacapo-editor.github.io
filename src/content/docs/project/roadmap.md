Partita is in early development. This page summarizes the plan; the detailed, always up-to-date list lives in [ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/partita-notation/partita/blob/main/docs/ROADMAP.md) on GitHub.

## Works today

- Multi-staff scores (treble, bass, alto and tenor clefs) with four voices per staff.
- Time and key signatures that can change mid-score.
- Note entry with the keyboard or the mouse, chords, rests, accidentals and transposition.
- Word-processor editing: caret, selection across staves, cut/copy/paste, grouped undo and redo.
- Ties, slurs, articulations and dynamics.
- Page, wrap and continuous views, zoom, printing, light and dark themes.
- Measure rests, multi-measure rests, and measures with too many beats flagged in red.
- Playback with recorded instruments, following ties, articulations and dynamics.
- An instrument for every staff (128 sounds), with its name at the start and a short name on later systems.
- Transposing instruments (B♭ clarinet and trumpet, F horn, saxophones…), written in their own key.
- Brackets and braces for instrument families and multi-staff instruments.
- Lyrics, up to eight verses.
- MusicXML import and export (`.musicxml`, `.xml`, `.mxl`).
- Several documents open at once, in tabs.
- Crash recovery that brings back unsaved work and its undo history.

## Coming next

- **Notation**: tuplets, grace notes, clef changes, hairpins, tempo marks and text.
- **Workflow**: a new score wizard with ensemble templates (string quartet, band, orchestra), insert and overwrite modes, and a note property panel.
- **Orchestra**: a concert-pitch view, nested brackets, and part extraction.
- **Formats**: PDF export and recent files.
- **Sound**: volume and pan per staff, metronome and count-in, MIDI keyboard input.
- **Interface**: customizable shortcuts and a Spanish translation.

## Further ahead

- **Version history**: go back to earlier states of a score, for example how it looked yesterday.
- Chord symbols and guitar tablature.
